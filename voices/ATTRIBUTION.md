# Voice attribution

House voices are Pocket TTS voice embeddings made from short clips of
[LibriTTS-R](https://www.openslr.org/141/) (Koizumi et al., 2023),
licensed CC BY 4.0. LibriTTS-R is derived from LibriTTS / LibriSpeech,
whose source recordings are public-domain LibriVox audiobooks.

`catalog.json` lists the exact speaker and utterance behind every voice.
The original clips are in `refs/`.

Speech model: [Kyutai Pocket TTS](https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts).
