Free text-to-speech API, voice cloning and AI music

The Free Engine gives you a free text-to-speech API with 60 natural voices, each in up to six moods: neutral, happy, sad, excited, calm and serious. Clone your own voice for free, keep it private or share it, and make free instrumental music from a text prompt.

Free plan

  • 5,000 characters of speech a day, up to 1,000 per request
  • 1 free voice clone per account, plus up to 4 extra mood clips
  • 3 instrumental music tracks a day, up to 30 seconds
  • MP3 or WAV output, simple REST API, no credit card

Voices

Moods

Ask for a mood with "mood": "excited". Each mood version was cloned from a real recording in that mood and kept only when the mood clearly comes through.

Quick start

curl https://thefreeengine.site/v1/tts -H "Authorization: Bearer $TFE_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"text":"Hello world!","voice":"aria","mood":"happy"}' -o hello.mp3

Use it from AI agents (MCP)

The Free Engine is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server at https://thefreeengine.site/mcp. Connect Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Kiro or any MCP client with your API key. Tools: text_to_speech, list_voices, make_music, music_status, voice_status, get_usage. MCP setup guide.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No card is needed. Premium plans with higher limits are coming soon.

How does voice cloning work?

Upload or record 10 to 20 seconds of clear speech. Free clones wait in a queue, and we email you when your voice is ready.

Which models power it?

Speech: Kyutai Pocket TTS. House voices: LibriTTS-R (CC BY 4.0). Music: Stability AI Stable Audio 3 Small.
